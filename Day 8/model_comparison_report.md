## Model Strengths and Weaknesses

### Logistic Regression
**Strengths:**
- Simple and interpretable
- Fast to train
- Good baseline classifier

**Weaknesses:**
- May not capture complex nonlinear relationships

**Use Cases:**
- Customer churn
- Employee attrition
- Medical classification

### Decision Tree
**Strengths:**
- Easy to interpret
- Handles nonlinear relationships

**Weaknesses:**
- Can overfit

**Use Cases:**
- Loan approval
- Risk classification
- Employee attrition

### Random Forest
**Strengths:**
- Strong predictive performance
- Handles nonlinear relationships
- More robust than a single tree

**Weaknesses:**
- Less interpretable
- Requires more computation

**Use Cases:**
- Fraud detection
- Customer churn
- Employee attrition

### KNN
**Strengths:**
- Simple and intuitive
- Easy to implement

**Weaknesses:**
- Sensitive to feature scaling
- Can be slower for large datasets

**Use Cases:**
- Pattern recognition
- Recommendation systems
- Small classification datasets

## Best Model

The best-performing model was selected based on the evaluation metrics, particularly F1 Score and ROC-AUC.

## Conclusion

The four classification algorithms were trained and evaluated on the employee attrition dataset. Their performance was compared using multiple evaluation metrics rather than accuracy alone.
